# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Gen X, Y, and Z Thais across income levels — from young professionals beginning to build wealth, to mid-career earners planning retirement, to established families managing assets. They arrive with financial anxiety or aspiration, often after a life event (career change, new family, inheritance). They are evaluating whether to trust a person with their money, not just buying a product. Desktop and mobile in equal measure.

## Product Purpose

CCPun is a personal financial advisory practice offering wealth planning, investment, and insurance services. The homepage exists to build trust fast — it must convince a stranger that CCPun is the right advisor before they ever speak to Pun. Success looks like a prospect booking a consultation or reaching out directly.

## Brand Personality

Trusted · Premium · Personal

CCPun occupies the space between a private wealth advisor and a trusted friend who happens to be an expert. Warm enough to be approachable across generations, authoritative enough to justify entrusting your financial future. Not a corporate institution. Not a startup. A specific, skilled human you want in your corner.

## Anti-references

- Corporate bank websites (SCB, Krungthai, GSB) — bureaucratic, cold, impersonal, designed for compliance not connection
- Generic Thai insurance agent sites — amateur layouts, stock photos, no design identity, low trust signals
- Ultra-luxury lifestyle brands (Bulgari, Hermès-style) — aspirational to the point of untouchable; CCPun must feel reachable

## Design Principles

1. **Personal over institutional** — every design decision should feel like it came from a skilled individual, not a committee. Warmth is not decoration; it is the product.
2. **Authority through clarity** — expertise is shown by making complex things simple, not by adding more jargon or data. If a section needs a paragraph to explain itself, redesign it.
3. **Premium without distance** — high quality in every detail, but never cold or exclusionary. The Warm Brown + Gold palette earns its place by feeling like quality, not wealth-signaling.
4. **One identity, not a template** — CCPun has no reference to copy. Each design choice must be justified against what CCPun is, not what financial sites "do."
5. **Trust compounds** — consistency across homepage, blog, and any future surface is the trust mechanism. Break visual consistency and you break the relationship.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Responsive with no device bias — desktop and mobile treated equally. Thai language primary; all copy must read naturally in Thai, not translated from English patterns.
